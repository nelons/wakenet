### What do we collect ?

This application does not collect any information and does not transmit any information to us or third parties.

All the configuration information for devices, groups and Active Directory is stored in the profile of your Windows user, and this is configured to 'roam' to other Windows devices if you use them.

Your IP address may be used to identify you when discovering network devices - please check with your network administrator to find out how this information will be used.

### How do we use it ?

We do not use this information in any way.
